How To Play

Learn the six major IoT components before beginning your investigations.

Sensor

Collects information from the environment.

Connectivity

Transmits data between devices and systems.

Processing Unit

Analyses information and makes decisions.

Cloud Platform

Stores, manages and presents IoT data.

Actuator

Performs a physical action.

User Interface

Allows people to view and control a system.
